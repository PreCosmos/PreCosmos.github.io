03 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PAGES

Hello, I’m Stavros Aeneas.
A world-builder at heart. A storyteller of lives in mind.

My stories move between myth, fantasy, and speculative fiction. Across Fantasia and Fantasia II, I explore how we connect, what we believe, and what happens when extraordinary power meets very human choices.

These worlds also have roots in game ideas and narrative design. This is a place to bring them together — and to invite you inside.
